## Display Mathematics

The Gaussian integral:

$$
\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} e^{-x^2}\,dx=\sqrt{\pi}
$$

A famous limit:

$$
\lim_{x\to0}\frac{\sin x}{x}=1
$$

The Pythagorean theorem:

$$
a^2+b^2=c^2
$$

A matrix:

$$
A=
\begin{bmatrix}
1 & 2\\
3 & 4
\end{bmatrix}
$$

A summation:

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n}k=\frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$
